What's in a ball

One record per colorway, one shape for every brand.

Twelve manufacturers publish specs twelve different ways. The API gives you one schema, with units and vocabularies normalized, and nulls where a manufacturer hasn't published something.

Identity

id · name · brand · color · status · release_date

Stable UUIDs you can key on. Each colorway is its own ball. status is current or retired, and retired balls stay in the catalog.

Specs per weight

weights[ ] · weight_lbs · rg · differential · mass_bias

RG and differential in inches for every weight the manufacturer lists, heaviest first. mass_bias (intermediate differential) for asymmetric cores.

Cover & core

coverstock{ name · material · type } · core{ name · type } · has_particle

Material is reactive_resin, urethane or polyester_plastic; type is solid, pearl or hybrid; cores are symmetric or asymmetric.

Finish

factory_finish · finish_category

The manufacturer's own finish text, plus a normalized category: polished, satin or dull.

Ball motion

plotter{ oil · motion · source }

Where the ball sits on a 16 × 18 oil-versus-motion chart, to 0.1, and whether that came from a manufacturer chart, our model, or a staff correction.

Links & media

image_url · manufacturer_url · learn_article_url · bowlerdepot_url

A CDN-hosted product photo, the manufacturer's page, and BowlerDepot's review and product pages when they exist.

Endpoints

Five endpoints. That's the whole surface.

Every response is JSON with "api_version": "1". Lists page with limit (default 200, max 500) and an opaque next_cursor.

EndpointReturns
GET/v1/changesThe sync feed. Balls created, changed or unpublished since your last cursor, as upsert or remove.
GET/v1/ballsEvery published ball, current and retired, paged by id. For a one-time full load.
GET/v1/balls/{id}One ball by id; 404 if it doesn't exist or isn't published.
GET/v1/brandsBrands with at least one published ball.
GET/v1/plotterEvery current ball's plotter position, plus similar balls from other brands and the nearest step toward more oil, less oil, more angular and smoother.
Staying in sync

Store a cursor. Ask what changed.

You don't re-download the catalog. Call /v1/changes with the cursor you saved last time, apply each upsert or remove by id, save the new cursor, and repeat until has_more is false. The first call with no cursor returns everything.

Changes appear about 60 seconds after they happen. A ball counts as changed when its fields, any weight's specs, its images or its review link change, not on every routine refresh. Each page carries a ball once, in its latest state, and re-applying a page is safe.

Every 15–60 minutes is plenty for most platforms.

sync.py 
cursor = load_cursor()          # None on the first run
while True:
    page = get("/v1/changes", cursor=cursor)
    for change in page["items"]:
        if change["change"] == "upsert":
            save(change["ball"])      # create or replace by id
        else:
            delete(change["id"])     # no longer published
    cursor = page["next_cursor"]
    save_cursor(cursor)         # after applying the page
    if not page["has_more"]:
        break
Ball motion plotter

Every current ball on one comparable chart.

Manufacturers publish motion charts on their own scales, if at all. We place every current ball on a single chart: oil from 1.0 (light) to 16.0 (heavy) and motion from 1.0 (smooth) to 18.0 (angular), to a tenth.

Positions read from a manufacturer's published chart are marked chart; the rest come from our model of the cover, finish, core and price (estimated), and staff corrections are manual. /v1/plotter adds the closest equivalents from other brands and the nearest ball in each direction, which is what powers "what should I try next?" features.

Access, limits & versioning

Boring, on purpose.

One key per platform, sent as a bearer token. Keys are issued by us, attributable per partner, and revocable individually. Keep yours server-side.

v1 only grows. We may add optional fields and endpoints, so ignore what you don't recognise. We will never rename, remove or retype a field, or change its units or meaning, inside v1. Anything breaking ships as /v2, and v1 keeps running while you migrate. The v1 contract is locked by automated tests.
